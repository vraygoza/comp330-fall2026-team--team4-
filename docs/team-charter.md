# Team Mission

Our mission is to operate as a professional software engineering team by maintaining clear communication, accountability, quality standards, and organized engineering evidence while collaboratively developing a successful project throughout the semester.

---

# Team Goals

- Successfully complete all COMP 330 assignments and project checkpoints.
- Maintain clear communication and accountability among team members.
- Establish and follow a professional GitHub workflow.
- Ensure all project deliverables are reviewed and documented.
- Keep repository evidence organized and up to date.
- Collaborate effectively to maintain project direction and progress throughout the semester.
- Deliver high-quality work through planning, review, testing, and teamwork.

# Communication Plan

## Primary Communication Channels

- WhatsApp
- Loyola Outlook Email

## Response Expectations

- Team members should respond within 24 hours during weekdays.
- Team members should notify the team if they anticipate any delays or extended unavailability.

## Urgent / Blocking Issues

Any issue that may prevent a team member from completing assigned work or impact a project deadline must be marked:

🚨 **BLOCKER**

Team members should respond as soon as possible so issues can be addressed before impacting project milestones or assignment deadlines.

---

# Meeting Schedule

- Formal team meetings will be held before major assignment deadlines and project checkpoints.
- Meetings will typically occur on the Wednesday before an assignment or checkpoint is due.
- Standard meeting time: Wednesday at 7:00 PM.
- Meeting duration: approximately 15-30 minutes.
- Team members will communicate throughout the week through WhatsApp and Outlook as needed.
- Additional meetings may be scheduled if significant blockers, risks, or project updates arise.

---

# Internal Deadline Policy

To ensure quality and allow time for review, all assigned work must be completed and submitted to the repository at least **48 hours (2 days)** before the official course deadline.

The final 48-hour review period will be used to:

- Verify required artifacts are complete.
- Review documentation for accuracy and consistency.
- Address missing information or repository issues.
- Complete pull request reviews and approvals.
- Verify assignment requirements have been satisfied.
- Prepare the final submission package.

Team members who anticipate delays must notify the team immediately so adjustments can be made before the internal deadline.

# Definition of Done

A task is considered complete when:

- Required work has been finished.
- Documentation has been updated if necessary.
- Changes have been committed to GitHub.
- Work has been reviewed by the appropriate team member.
- Any identified issues have been addressed.
- Assignment requirements have been met.
- The work is ready for submission.

---

# Success Criteria

The team is considered prepared for a checkpoint when:

- All team members understand their responsibilities.
- Communication channels are active and consistently used.
- Required discussions have occurred before the checkpoint deadline.
- Assigned work has been completed by the internal deadline.
- Repository documentation is updated and committed.
- Required reviews have been completed.
- Engineering evidence is organized and easy to locate.
- Final review is completed at least 48 hours before the official due date.

# Team Commitment
