## Our Project
We're building a calorie-counter bot on Telegram. Users text the bot what they ate, and it keeps track of their calories. The first version is small: log a meal and see your daily total. It will be tested by about 10 to 15 friends and classmates. Tracking macros (protein, carbs, fats, fiber) comes later.

## How We Plan Each Assignment
1. **Plan:** Break the assignment into tasks. Every task gets one owner and a due date.
2. **Build:** Owners do their work on a branch and open a pull request.
3. **Review:** In the 48 hours before the due date, teammates review each other's work and fix problems.
4. **Submit:** Victoria does the final check and submits to Sakai.
5. **Look back:** At the next meeting we go over what worked and what to change.

Tasks are tracked as GitHub Issues so everyone can see who owns what.

## Keeping Scope Under Control
- The scope for each cycle is locked at the start of that cycle.
- New feature ideas go into a GitHub Issue as backlog, not into the current cycle.
- Any change to scope is agreed on by the team and recorded in the Decision Log.
