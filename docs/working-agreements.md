# Team 4 Working Agreements

## Purpose

This document establishes the expectations, responsibilities, and working practices that Team 4 agrees to follow throughout the COMP 330 project.

---

# Communication Expectations

- Primary communication methods:
  - WhatsApp
  - Loyola Outlook Email

- Team members are expected to respond within 24 hours during weekdays.

- Team members should communicate anticipated absences, delays, or scheduling conflicts as early as possible.

- Questions and concerns should be raised promptly to avoid project delays.

---

# Internal Deadline Policy

Team 4 will maintain an internal deadline that is 48 hours before the official assignment or checkpoint due date.

This review period allows the team to:

- Review documentation
- Correct mistakes
- Verify repository organization
- Complete reviews
- Address missing requirements
- Prepare final submissions

---

# Meeting Expectations

- Formal meetings will be held before major assignment deadlines and project checkpoints.
- Standard meeting time is Wednesday at 7:00 PM.
- Meetings will generally last 15-30 minutes.
- Additional meetings may be scheduled when necessary.

---

# Blockers and Escalation

Issues that may impact deadlines should be identified immediately.

Urgent issues should be labeled:

 **BLOCKER**

The team will work together to resolve blockers before they affect assignment submissions.

---

# Repository Expectations

Team members are responsible for:

- Committing work regularly.
- Keeping documentation current.
- Organizing files in the correct repository locations.
- Following established repository structure.
- Reviewing assigned materials before submission.

---

# Definition of Done

A task is considered complete when:

- Assigned work is finished.
- Documentation has been updated.
- Changes have been committed to GitHub.
- Work has been reviewed when necessary.
- Assignment requirements have been satisfied.
- The work is ready for submission.

---

# Professional Conduct

Team members agree to:

- Communicate respectfully.
- Complete assigned responsibilities.
- Contribute fairly to team efforts.
- Provide assistance when teammates need support.
- Maintain professionalism throughout the project.

---

# Team Agreement

By participating in Team 4, members agree to follow these working agreements and contribute to the team's success throughout the semester.
